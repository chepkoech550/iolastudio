03 / The studio

Made with

Iola Studio is an independent art and product practice founded by Viola Korir. We explore human experiences through objects, illustration, storytelling and design.

Our work moves between practical tools, expressive products and creative collaborations. Each idea begins with a question: how might this become useful in someone's life?
